What it is

oriz Play is a free creative-play studio that runs entirely in your browser. Roll story dice, spin writing prompts, and play would-you-rather / this-or-that — then optionally expand any roll into a vivid story opening with AI, and share the result by link.

Key features

  • Story Dice — 1-9 categorized dice (Character, Setting, Object, Goal, Obstacle, Mood, Twist, Sense, Wildcard).
  • Prompt Roller — recombines fragments into a one-line writing prompt.
  • Would You Rather — impossible dilemmas to spark a story.
  • This or That — quickfire either/or picks.
  • Expand with AI — fuse any roll into a story opening (optional, keyless).
  • Fresh prompt pack — five brand-new prompts on any theme.
  • Share by link — a compressed permalink rebuilds the exact roll.

Tech

  • Client-only Astro (static) + React 19 islands + Tailwind v4.
  • g4f AI via @chirag127/oz-ai — keyless multi-provider, automatic failover.
  • PWA-installable — offline app shell.
  • 100% in-browser — no upload, no signup, no tracking.

100% client-side. Every roll happens in your browser; nothing is uploaded and there is no account. AI expansion is optional polish through a keyless gateway — if it is down, the dice still roll.
